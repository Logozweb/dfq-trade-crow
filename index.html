DFQ Trade Crow Refund, Inspection & Escrow Policy
Effective Date: November 2026 • Applies to all Barter, Cash-Top-Up, Spotlight Boosts & Pawn Transactions
1. Peer-to-Peer Direct Barter & Cash Swaps

DFQ Trade Crow operates as a peer-to-peer exchange marketplace. For direct in-person swaps completed outside Escrow Hold, traders are required to inspect items thoroughly (including Serial/IMEI verification and physical testing) before handing over goods or cash. Once both parties confirm physical handover in person, direct peer-to-peer swaps are final unless misrepresentation or stolen property is proven.

2. Escrow Hold & Inspection Refund Guarantee (100% Refundable Before Release)

When a cash top-up or purchase is placed under DFQ Inspection Escrow Hold, funds are safeguarded until the receiving party inspects and approves the item within the agreed 24-to-48-hour inspection window:

  • Full Refund Eligibility: If the delivered item is significantly not as described, defective, locked (e.g., iCloud/FRP locked), or fails Serial/IMEI verification, the buyer/swapper may raise a dispute before releasing escrow to receive a 100% refund of the escrowed amount upon verified return of the item.
  • Cancelled Trades Before Dispatch: If either party cancels a trade before physical dispatch or meetup, any escrowed cash top-up is refunded in full within 1–3 business days.
3. Digital Pawn Tickets & Buyback Window

Pawn transactions carry a fixed 14, 21, or 30-day buyback window with a transparent 10% service fee stated upfront on the printable Pawn Ticket (ticket.php). If a pawned item is redeemed on or before the due date, full ownership returns immediately upon buyback settlement. Pawn service fees are non-refundable once the cash advance has been disbursed.

4. Spotlight Boost Credits & Platform Fees

Spotlight Boost credits used to feature listings at the top of the marketplace are non-refundable once activated on a live listing, except where a technical outage prevented the listing from displaying.

Terms & Conditions of Use
Governing Law: Federal Republic of Nigeria • Platform Operator: DFQ Trade Crow
1. Eligibility & Family Supervision

While minors and families may browse listings on DFQ Trade Crow, creating an account, executing binding barter contracts, submitting NIN/BVN KYC verification, or entering pawn agreements is strictly restricted to individuals aged 18 years and older (or minors trading strictly under the direct supervision and verified account of a parent or legal guardian).

2. Lawful Ownership & Accurate Listings

By posting an item on DFQ Trade Crow, you warrant that you are the sole lawful owner of the item, that it is free of undisclosed liens, theft reports, or carrier/cloud locks, and that all photos, battery health metrics, mileage, and descriptions are truthful.

3. Prohibited Items

The following items are strictly banned from DFQ Trade Crow: stolen or untraceable electronics, counterfeit currency or documents, firearms, ammunition, explosives, illicit drugs, hazardous chemicals, wildlife products, and adult/explicit materials. Violating accounts are permanently suspended and reported to law enforcement.

4. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a trade dispute, both parties agree to submit transaction logs, chat records, Pawn Ticket IDs, and Serial/IMEI evidence to DFQ Trade Crow Moderation for good-faith mediation prior to initiating external legal proceedings in Lagos State, Nigeria.

Privacy & Data Protection Policy (NDPR Compliant)
Aligned with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) & NDPR Standards
1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to operate a safe peer-to-peer marketplace: your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, city/area location, uploaded listing photos, optional purchase receipts, and device Serial/IMEI numbers.

2. How NIN, BVN & Phone OTP Data Are Protected

When you verify your trader identity via NIN, BVN, or Phone SMS OTP, your verification number is used strictly to confirm identity authenticity and generate a masked confirmation status (e.g., 221*****894). We never sell, rent, or publicly display your full NIN, BVN, or raw password to other users.

3. Cookies & Local Preferences

DFQ Trade Crow uses essential session cookies and browser storage to keep you logged in, remember your Dark/Light theme choice, and store your Saved Favorites Watchlist.

4. Your Data Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account data and uploaded receipts at any time via your Trade Desk or by contacting our Data Protection Officer at legal@dfqtradecrow.com.

Anti-Theft, Serial/IMEI & Digital Pawn Compliance
Zero-Tolerance Stolen Goods & Transparent Buyback Charter
1. Mandatory Serial / IMEI & Receipt Screening

All smartphones, laptops, consoles, generators, and vehicles listed for Pawn or high-value swap are subject to Serial Number / IMEI / VIN lookup. Any device flagged as lost or stolen is immediately locked from trading and its audit trail preserved for law enforcement verification.

2. Digital Pawn Ticket Transparency

Every pawn agreement generates a timestamped Digital Pawn Ticket recording the principal advance, the flat 10% fee, the exact maturity date, and serial identifiers—protecting both the pawner's right to redeem and the lender's collateral security.

3. Default & Collateral Liquidation

If a pawner fails to redeem or request a mutually agreed extension before the expiration of the 14, 21, or 30-day hold window, the collateral item may be relisted for outright sale to recover the principal advance.

Safe Meetup & Community Safety Guidelines
Protecting Traders, Parents & Young Visitors
1. Always Meet in Well-Lit Public Places

Conduct physical swaps and inspections during daylight hours at busy, CCTV-monitored public locations such as shopping malls, bank premises, or verified hub spots. Never invite strangers to private residences for unverified deals.

2. Test Before You Hand Over

Check IMEI numbers (*#06#), verify iCloud/Google account sign-out, test battery charging, and confirm bank transfers inside your own banking app (never rely solely on SMS alerts) before releasing your item.

3. Youth & Family Protection

Parents and guardians must accompany any minor under 18 during physical item pickups or swaps. Report any suspicious listing or behavior immediately using the red Flag icon on any listing modal.
